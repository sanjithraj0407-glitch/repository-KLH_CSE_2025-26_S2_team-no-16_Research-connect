Research Questions and Answers - Cybersecurity

Question: What is cybersecurity?
Answer: Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access and attacks.

Question: What is encryption?
Answer: Encryption converts readable information into a protected form so that unauthorized users cannot easily understand it.

Question: What is malware?
Answer: Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information, or perform unauthorized activities.

Question: What is phishing?
Answer: Phishing is a technique that attempts to trick users into revealing sensitive information through fraudulent messages or websites.

Question: What is authentication?
Answer: Authentication verifies the identity of a user before allowing access to a system.

Question: Why is cybersecurity important?
Answer: Cybersecurity helps protect confidential information, computer systems, networks, and digital services.
