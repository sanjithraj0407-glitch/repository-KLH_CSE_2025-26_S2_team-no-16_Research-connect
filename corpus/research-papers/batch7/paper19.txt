Research Questions and Answers - Pattern Searching

Question: What is pattern searching?
Answer: Pattern searching is the process of finding a specific sequence of characters or words inside a larger text.

Question: What is the KMP algorithm?
Answer: KMP is a string pattern matching algorithm that uses information about previously matched characters to search efficiently.

Question: What is a text corpus?
Answer: A text corpus is a collection of text documents that can be analyzed or searched.

Question: Why is pattern searching useful?
Answer: Pattern searching helps users find relevant words, phrases, and information inside large collections of documents.

Question: What is an occurrence?
Answer: An occurrence is one location where the searched pattern appears inside a text.

Question: What are pattern positions?
Answer: Pattern positions are the character indexes where a searched pattern starts in a document.

Question: How does KMP improve pattern searching?
Answer: KMP avoids unnecessary comparisons by using a prefix table to determine where the next comparison should begin.
