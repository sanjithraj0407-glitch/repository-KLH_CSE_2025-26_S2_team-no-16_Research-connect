Research Questions and Answers - Information Retrieval

Question: What is information retrieval?
Answer: Information retrieval is the process of finding relevant information from a collection of documents.

Question: What is a search query?
Answer: A search query is a word or phrase entered by a user to find relevant information.

Question: What is a research corpus?
Answer: A research corpus is a collection of research documents used for searching and analysis.

Question: How can pattern searching help researchers?
Answer: Pattern searching can quickly locate specific words or phrases inside research documents.

Question: What makes a search system useful?
Answer: A useful search system should provide relevant results quickly and clearly.

Question: Why are search results important?
Answer: Search results help users identify documents containing the information they are looking for.

Question: What information can a search system display?
Answer: A search system can display matching documents, occurrence counts, and the positions where patterns occur.

Question: How does this project search research papers?
Answer: This project loads research-paper text files into a corpus and uses the KMP algorithm to search for user-entered patterns.
