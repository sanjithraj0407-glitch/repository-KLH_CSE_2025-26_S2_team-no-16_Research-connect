Research question and answer systems help users retrieve useful information from large collections of documents.
A research question defines the main problem that a study attempts to investigate.
An answer provides information that addresses the research question using evidence from relevant sources.
Pattern searching can help identify questions, answers, keywords, and important terms in research documents.
Question answering systems use natural language processing to understand user queries.
Information retrieval techniques help users find relevant documents from a research corpus.
KMP pattern searching can locate exact patterns inside text documents efficiently.
