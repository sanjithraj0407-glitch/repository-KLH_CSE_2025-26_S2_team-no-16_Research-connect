Cybersecurity protects computer systems, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access.
Modern cybersecurity uses encryption, authentication, firewalls, and intrusion detection systems.
Network security is important for protecting organizations from cyber attacks and data breaches.
Encryption converts sensitive information into a secure format that cannot be easily understood by unauthorized users.
Cybersecurity researchers study malware, phishing, ransomware, and other security threats.
Artificial intelligence and machine learning are increasingly used to detect suspicious activities.
Strong passwords and multi-factor authentication can improve account security.
